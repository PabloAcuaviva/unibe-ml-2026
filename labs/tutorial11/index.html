3. MM: the median

To minimize $f(\theta)=\sum_i|x_i-\theta|$, each step puts a parabola $g(\theta\mid\theta_t)$ above $f$ that touches it at $\theta_t$, then jumps to the bottom of the parabola: the weighted mean with weights $w_i=1/|x_i-\theta_t|$. Drag the black dot to choose $\theta_0$, or drag a data point.
