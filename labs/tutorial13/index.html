2. Value iteration

A grid world. The agent moves up, right, down or left, and a move into the border or a grey block leaves it where it is. Two corner cells give the rewards $+10$ and $+2$, two cells give $-5$, and all others $0$. Each sweep sets $V(s):=R(s)+\gamma\max_a V(s')$, where $s'$ is the cell that $a$ leads to. The arrows show the greedy action; a red arrow is not optimal.
